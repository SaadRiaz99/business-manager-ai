# BizPilot AI Selling Plan

## Verdict

**Hot idea: 8/10 when niche-focused; 4/10 as a generic business manager.** CRM is competitive, so the winning angle is a narrow promise: “AI lead pipeline for small marketing agencies.” The product becomes valuable when it saves follow-up time or helps win a client—not merely because it has AI.

## Ideal first customer

- Marketing or web agency with 2–15 people
- Gets leads through referrals, forms, or manual prospecting
- Currently manages leads in spreadsheets or chat messages
- Owner forgets follow-ups or cannot see the pipeline clearly
- Can afford $49–$149 per month

## Simple packages

| Plan | Offer | Price |
|---|---|---:|
| Pilot | Setup, branding, 50 imported leads, 7-day support | $100 once |
| Starter | CRM, scoring, drafts, tasks, 1 user | $49/month |
| Growth | Automation, integrations, reports, 5 users | $149/month |

## Demo script

1. Ask the prospect how they currently track leads.
2. Add one example lead using their actual target niche.
3. Show why the system scored it hot, warm, or cold.
4. Generate a personalized outreach draft.
5. Move the lead through the pipeline and schedule a follow-up.
6. Show the dashboard’s pipeline value and overdue actions.
7. Offer a paid seven-day pilot, not an unlimited free build.

## Outreach message

Hi {{first_name}}, I noticed {{agency_name}} helps {{their_niche}} businesses. I built a simple lead-management system for small agencies that scores prospects, drafts personalized outreach, and keeps follow-ups from being missed. I prepared a short demo using your type of customer. Would you be open to a 10-minute walkthrough this week?

## Daily client acquisition routine

- Select one city and one agency niche.
- Research 20 suitable agencies using legitimate public sources.
- Personalize the opening line for each business.
- Send a maximum of 20 thoughtful messages—not bulk spam.
- Follow up once after three days and once after seven days.
- Record every reply, objection, and demo outcome.
- Improve the product only from repeated customer requests.

## Validation goal

Do not build every integration first. Get 10 demonstrations, 3 paid pilots, and 1 recurring customer. If prospects repeatedly decline, record why. Change the niche or offer before adding more features.

## Important production rules

- Use approved data providers and respect their terms.
- Store only necessary business contact information.
- Keep a human approval step before sending messages.
- Provide unsubscribe handling and follow applicable email/privacy laws.
- Never represent fictional demo leads as verified real prospects.
